Does the progression logic feel right?

This lets you log pretend sessions for one exercise and see exactly how the app would change your next target. The rules: double progression (add reps until every set hits the top of the range, then add weight), hold the weight if the last set went to failure (RIR 0), a stall after 3 sessions with no improvement in weight or average reps per set, then a ~10% deload. Missing the bottom of the range mid-session offers a one-tap drop that doesn't change the target. Try the scenarios on the right and look for anything that feels wrong.
